# Tebex-Cord

Tebex-Cord is a simple yet powerful Discord bot built with Discord.js that integrates with the Tebex Headless API, enabling seamless interaction with your Tebex Game Webstore.

### Key Features:
- **User Authentication**: Users are prompted to log in with their username and are automatically logged out every 24 hours for security.
- **Package Management**: Easily add or remove packages, gift cards, coupons, and creator codes directly from Discord.
- **Checkout Links**: Generate direct checkout links powered by the Tebex Payment Gateway, ensuring smooth transactions.
- **In-Game Command Support**: Any in-game commands associated with package purchases will trigger as expected, just like on your webstore.
- **Payment Notifications**: Receive real-time payment notifications logged directly in Discord for easy tracking.

With Tebex-Cord, you can manage your Tebex store and enhance your Discord community experience effortlessly.
